# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community for everyone participating in Project Wifi UIT, regardless of background, identity, experience, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Welcome questions and explain decisions clearly.
- Protect privacy: do not share patient information, credentials, or other sensitive data.
- Be mindful that this project concerns hospital contexts; do not make unsupported clinical claims or encourage unsafe use.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, doxxing, publication of private information, and knowingly unsafe or deceptive technical claims are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Include the relevant context and any supporting links, while avoiding sensitive information. Maintainers may remove content, request changes, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This code of conduct applies in project spaces and whenever an individual is representing the project.
